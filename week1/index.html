SAFETY GUIDE

Know What To Do During a Disaster

🏚️

Earthquake

Drop, cover and hold on. Stay away from windows and heavy objects.

🌊

Flood

Move to higher ground and avoid walking or driving through flood water.

⛰️

Landslide

Move away from unstable slopes and follow official evacuation instructions.

🔥

Fire

Leave the building immediately and call emergency services.
